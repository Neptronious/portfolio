07 Design Principles

Five rules that drove action.

The lens every decision was tested against — from the headline number down to a single item on a shelf.

01

Lead with the answer

Open the dashboard and immediately see where waste is worst and what it's costing. The headline does the work — no hunting, no maths.

02

Insight, then action

Every surfaced problem carries a suggested next step and a potential saving. Data becomes a to-do list, not a report to decode.

03

Let people drill down

From market → store → department → fineline → item. A clear path from the big number to the exact cause behind it.

04

Set goals & track progress

Targets, store rank and a leaderboard turn abstract reduction into something a team can aim at — and feel good about hitting.

05

Reduce clutter ruthlessly

Show the few things that matter now; keep everything else one tap away. Signal over volume, at every level.
